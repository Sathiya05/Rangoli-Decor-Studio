Good to know

Questions we get every week

How early should I book for Diwali? +

Festival mornings book out 3–4 weeks ahead. For wedding dates we recommend confirming 6 weeks ahead, and Diwali-week home slots usually close by the end of August.

What materials do you actually use? +

Rice flour, sieved stone powder, dry vermilion or turmeric for reds, indigo and sandalwood for cool tones, plus fresh petals, banana leaves and terracotta diyas for festive finishes. Zero chemicals, safe on marble.

Do you travel outside Bengaluru? +

Yes — Hyderabad, Chennai, and Mysuru are regular routes, and our team travels anywhere in India for destination weddings. Travel and stay are quoted transparently before you confirm.

Can I keep the design and have it redrawn later? +

Absolutely. Every approved design is saved as a scaled grid plan in our archive, so a festival re-draw years later matches the original line for line.
