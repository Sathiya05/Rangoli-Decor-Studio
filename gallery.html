What the captions tell you

Surface

Every caption lists the footprint in feet. Floor pitch is calculated from that number, so a 4 ft and a 20 ft version of the same design are not simply scaled copies.

Material

“Powder” means rice flour and sieved stone powder. “Petals” or “flowers” means loose fresh material on a wet base — it will not survive a full day of footfall without a refresh.

Time

Installation time scales roughly with area and complexity. Add an hour for every extra 10 ft, and another hour if the design has to be redrawn around furniture.
