Payment

50% to confirm the date, balance on completion. Retainers are billed on the 1st of each month.

02

Rescheduling

Free up to 48 hours before. Inside 48 hours on an event booking, 30% of the value is retained.

03

Cancellations

Retainers can be paused for one month per year at no charge, with 14 days’ notice.

04

Longevity

Powder floors last 3–5 days indoors. Petal floors last through one day of footfall, refreshed free on day two.

Package questions

Can I mix packages, e.g. Starter plus Signature? +

Yes. Most homes run Home Starter through the year and add Festive Signature in October. The monthly retainer is adjusted by the days of the festive week that fall inside it.

Is a home visit needed before booking? +

It helps, but it is not mandatory. Within 15 km of the studio the visit is free; further out we deduct the visit cost from the booking if you proceed.

Do you supply the oil and cotton for the diyas? +

Diyas and wicks are included in the add-on price. Cooking oil and lighting the lamps are left to you, or we can arrange a helper for an hour at ₹600.

Can corporate work be invoiced with GST? +

Yes. We issue a proper GST invoice with our GSTIN, plus vendor onboarding documents, W-9 equivalents and installation reports your finance team can file.
